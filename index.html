Blues Lab

Born in the Deep South over a century ago, the blues shaped rock, jazz and pop. Learn how it works, then play with it.

Version 9: 6 activities and 18 quiz questions

Activities

1. Blues scale

Tap the keys to hear the C blues scale. Six different notes, then back to the top C.

Challenge: one of these is the “blue note”, the bent, sad-sounding one. Which?

2. Build a 12 bar blues

Tap each bar to cycle through the chords C, F and G. These are the three main chords of C major: C = I, F = IV and G = V. Hint: it starts with four bars of C and finishes with C (or G) in the last bar.

3. Rhythm lab

Press play on all four rhythms. A quiet click marks each beat. Watch where the gold dots land compared with the click lines.

4. Instrument match

Match each blues instrument to what it does.

Quiz time

Pick a topic quiz to practise one key term, or take the full quiz to complete this activity.

Jazz band challenge

Which of these instruments work well in a jazz band? Tap every one you think belongs, then check your band. Watch out, some don’t belong!
